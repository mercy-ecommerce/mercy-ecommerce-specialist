MY PROCESS

From Understanding the Business to Identifying Better Opportunities.

Good work starts with understanding the business before deciding what needs to change. I look at the customer experience, product presentation, offers, and conversion journey to understand what is happening and where meaningful opportunities may exist.

Understand

We begin by understanding the business, its customers, objectives, current position, and the areas you want to improve.

Examine

I examine the relevant parts of the customer journey, product experience, offers, messaging, and conversion points to understand what may be helping or limiting the buying experience.

Identify

I turn those observations into clear opportunities, focusing on areas where greater clarity, stronger presentation, or a better customer experience could make a difference.

Recommend

I provide practical recommendations based on what was identified, giving you a clearer idea of what to focus on and what the appropriate next step could be.

The goal is not to change everything. It is to understand what matters, identify where opportunities exist, and focus on the actions that make the most sense for the business.

Demonstration Work & Strategic Thinking

PROJECT 01

Sales Conversion Review

Exploring how a visitor moves through an online store and identifying points where purchasing momentum may become weaker.

The Situation

A store can attract visitors without every visitor understanding what to do next. This demonstration examines the customer journey from the first interaction through consideration and toward the point of action.

What I Looked At

  • First impression and initial clarity
  • Navigation and product discovery
  • Value communication
  • Calls to action
  • Trust and purchasing confidence

What I Identified

The demonstration focuses on moments where visitors may have to think too much, search for important information, or lack enough confidence to continue toward a purchase.

Recommended Direction

Recommendations would focus on creating a more intentional journey by improving clarity, strengthening important messages, reducing unnecessary friction, and making the next action easier to understand.

POTENTIAL BUSINESS VALUE

A clearer customer journey can help create a more focused buying experience and give existing store traffic a stronger opportunity to move toward action.

PROJECT 02

Product Page Improvement

Examining how product information, presentation, value, trust, and calls to action work together around the buying decision.

The Situation

A product page has an important job: helping the customer understand what is being offered and giving them enough confidence to consider taking the next step.

What I Looked At

  • Product presentation and visual hierarchy
  • Value proposition
  • Product information
  • Trust-building elements
  • Call-to-action clarity

What I Identified

The demonstration looks for areas where the customer may still have unanswered questions, unclear expectations, or insufficient context before making a purchasing decision.

Recommended Direction

The recommended direction is to make important information easier to find, communicate value more clearly, strengthen confidence, and create a more natural path toward the purchase action.

POTENTIAL BUSINESS VALUE

Better product communication can help customers make more informed decisions and create a stronger experience around the product being considered.

PROJECT 03

Offer Presentation Strategy

Looking at how an offer communicates value and whether the customer has enough context to understand the opportunity being presented.

The Situation

An offer can be valuable while still being difficult for a customer to understand. This demonstration explores how the structure and presentation of an offer can influence clarity and perceived value.

What I Looked At

  • Offer structure
  • Value communication
  • Customer relevance
  • Supporting information
  • Action and next-step clarity

What I Identified

The review considers whether the customer can quickly understand what the offer includes, why it matters, and what makes the opportunity relevant to them.

Recommended Direction

The direction would focus on making the offer easier to understand, improving the hierarchy of information, communicating value with greater clarity, and creating a stronger connection between the offer and the customer's needs.

POTENTIAL BUSINESS VALUE

Clearer offer presentation can make it easier for customers to understand the opportunity and make a more informed decision about whether it is right for them.
